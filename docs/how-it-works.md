# How It Works

Each session follows a clear four-step cycle:

1. **Read and Listen** — Explore books, passages, and podcasts.
2. **Understand and Explain** — Summarize ideas and share understanding.
3. **Speak and Interact** — Practice vocabulary, dialogues, and debates.
4. **Evaluate and Improve** — Receive feedback and work on specific areas.

This cycle keeps practice structured without making sessions rigid.
