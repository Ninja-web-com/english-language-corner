# Learning Goals

The English Language Corner focuses on six core goals:

1. **Reading comprehension** — Understand written texts and express meaning in your own words.
2. **Listening comprehension** — Follow spoken English and capture key ideas from podcasts and talks.
3. **Vocabulary development** — Learn new words, their meanings, usage, and pronunciation.
4. **Speaking fluency and confidence** — Express ideas clearly in dialogues, debates, and short talks.
5. **Pronunciation and grammar** — Refine accuracy through practice and targeted correction.
6. **Constructive feedback and communication** — Give and receive useful feedback that supports growth.

These goals guide every session so progress stays focused and meaningful.
