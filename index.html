Our Activities

Practice that builds real skill

Five focused activities designed to strengthen reading, listening, vocabulary, speaking, and constructive feedback.

Reading and Explanation

Students read a passage from a physical book and explain what they understood in their own words while others listen and ask questions.

Podcast Listening and Explanation

Students listen to informative English podcasts on topics such as AI, technology, and current affairs, then summarize and explain the main ideas.

Vocabulary of the Day/Week

Students learn new English words, understand their meanings and usage, practice pronunciation, and use them in their own sentences.

Debates and Dialogues

Students participate in debates on relevant topics and practice dialogues based on real-life situations to improve fluency, confidence, and communication skills.

Speaker Evaluation and Feedback

Students deliver short speeches or explanations, while peers and the facilitator evaluate their clarity, fluency, pronunciation, vocabulary, and grammar, providing constructive feedback for improvement.

Learning Goals

What we aim to strengthen

Clear goals guide every session so progress stays focused and meaningful.

01

Reading comprehension

Understand written texts and express meaning in your own words.

02

Listening comprehension

03

Vocabulary development

Learn new words, their meanings, usage, and pronunciation.

04

Speaking fluency and confidence

05

06
